## 4. Routing & Flight Strategy

**Concept:** SEA → **one Asia stopover** → mainland China → SEA, structured so the **4 US-passport travelers** can use China's **10-day (240-hour) visa-free transit** benefit.

- **Visa-free transit rule (critical):** the ticket must **arrive from one country/region and depart to a *different* third country/region** — hence the plan to **add a stop somewhere in Asia** in the middle.
- **Josh's constraint:** as a PRC-passport holder he **cannot use Taiwan** as the transit point without a permit → **the pre-China city must work for Josh** too.

### Stopover candidates
- **Singapore** — has a **direct flight from Seattle**, reportedly **very cheap in September (~$300)**.
- **Taipei (Taiwan)** — direct flight; interesting **but blocked for Josh** (permit issue).
- **Japan (Tokyo)** — direct flight; interesting.
- **Korea (Seoul)** — direct flight; interesting.

### China entry / internal routing ideas
- Enter China from the stopover city; visit **Shanghai** and/or **Chongqing**.
- **Hainan Airlines** option: **SEA → Chongqing (CKG) → Shanghai (PVG)**, very cheap; can select a **one-stop** and **extend the Chongqing→Shanghai leg to another city** — attractive routing. **Downside:** no Hainan airline points/credits on hand, so likely paid cash (unless booked via Alaska partner award).

### Cabin / comfort

- Will require **extra cash or points**.
