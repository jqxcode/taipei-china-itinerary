1. 🐟 Key premise: 2026 East China Sea fishing season opens at noon on 9/16 → schedule Taizhou after 9/16

  • Confirmed: the East China Sea fully opens for fishing at 12:00 on September 16, 2026 (the “small opening” on 8/5 already brings swimming crab/red shrimp; the full opening on 9/16 has the widest variety, freshest catch, and lowest prices). Sources: Zhejiang Online Taizhou channel, Taizhou government zjtz.gov.cn.
  • Therefore, Taizhou is best scheduled on 9/17–9/18 (the first full day after the full fishing-season opening) — the first fresh batches of wild East China Sea swimming crab 🦀, small yellow croaker, pomfret, ribbonfish, and mantis shrimp arrive in large quantities. Jiaojiang’s newly opened Yudu Fishing Town (葭沚街道, 140 stalls + 134 shops) in 2026-08 = a new landmark for seafood shopping/night-market eating.

3. 🐠 Taizhou 2 days / 1 night (elder-friendly · 9/17–9/18, after fishing opens)

Buy tickets to the right station: For Tiantai Mountain/Jiaojiang city area, buy “Taizhou Station (台州站)”; for Linhai Ancient City, buy “Linhai Station (临海站)” (direct via Hangzhou-Taizhou HSR) or transfer to S1 from Taizhou West Station.

Day1 (Thu 9/17): Hangzhou East (杭州东)→Taizhou (台州) (G7461 10:56→12:21, ¥136) - Afternoon: Guoqing Temple (Tiantai Mountain) (国清寺（天台山）): ancestral temple of the Tiantai school, free, flat ground with no steps, best suited for elders (chartered car about 45 minutes). - Shenxianju (Xianju) (神仙居（仙居）): take the South Gate cable car up (one-way about ¥80) to save 90% of the effort; boardwalks are gentle and shuttle buses are available; admission about ¥110 (half price for 65+). - Evening: Jiaojiang Yudu Fishing Town first-fresh-catch night market after fishing opens (opens 18:30; you can sit while choosing): wild swimming crab 🦀, small yellow croaker, pomfret, mantis shrimp (~¥70/jin). Stay in Jiaojiang city area.

Day2 (Fri 9/18): Taizhou (台州)→Shanghai (上海) - Morning: Linhai Taizhou Prefecture City Wall (Jiangnan Great Wall): enter from Xingshan Gate (兴善门) and walk the highlight section Ziyang Street (紫阳街)→Wangjiang Tower (望江楼), 2 km (do what feels manageable; you can climb only a few sections); admission about ¥55 (free/half price for elders). - Ziyang Ancient Street (紫阳古街) (Song-dynasty style, flat) snacks: seaweed pastry, jiaobingtong, egg-white “sheep tail,” wheat-oil pastry, black-rice mochi. - Afternoon return: Recommend going directly back to Shanghai G7585 Taizhou (台州) 17:20→Shanghai Hongqiao (上海虹桥) 19:41 (~2h21); or first return to Hangzhou East (杭州东) (~1h) and transfer. Stay in Shanghai Pudong (上海浦东) (near PVG).
