### Plan A【Most recommended · Flexible and independent, good for splitting up often】
- **Setup**: **2 Chunghwa Telecom physical SIMs** (buy on site at Taoyuan Airport).
- **Price**: 3-day unlimited data **NT$300/card (about US$10)**, includes a small calling credit and a local Taiwan +886 number.
- **Location**: Taoyuan **T1/T2 arrivals hall Chunghwa Telecom counter**, handle it right after customs; just show passports (**credit cards accepted**).
- **How it works**: 2 people carry SIM cards and can turn on hotspot anytime; the other 3 people connect to hotspot.
- **Pros**: ① if people shop separately / take MRT separately, each subgroup still has internet and is not dependent on one hotspot; ② physical SIMs are more stable than travel eSIMs and include a +886 number for restaurant bookings / taxis; ③ two cards total about **US$20**.
- ⚠️ **China-market Xiaomi cannot install eSIM, but physical SIMs work in all phones**, perfectly compatible with Apple + Xiaomi.

### Plan B【One ultra-simple package · Best if the group stays together the whole time】
- Rent a **Pocket WiFi** device, pick up and return at Taoyuan Airport, connects up to 5 devices at the same time.
- **Price**: about **US$22–28** for 3 days (booking online through Klook/KKday is cheaper).
- **Cons**: cannot separate too far (if you split up, some people have no internet); need to carry a power bank; for departure, **return it at the same location**, and if the flight is very late, confirm counter hours first.
- 👉 Only suitable for groups that almost never split up.

### Plan C【eSIM · Supplement only, with clear limitations】
- **Airalo Taiwan 3-day unlimited eSIM ≈US$12.5/card**.
- **Issue**: **China-market Xiaomi cannot install eSIM**; this only works if all phones are iPhones / overseas-version phones → China-market Xiaomi makes this route a direct pass.

### Final copy-this plan
- **Scenario 1｜You will split up (most common) → Plan A**: buy 2 Chunghwa Telecom 3-day unlimited cards at Taoyuan Airport (NT$300/card), after landing and clearing customs find the Chunghwa Telecom counter, and simply say “3 days tourist unlimited data prepaid SIM, 2 pieces”; after setup, turn on hotspot on 2 phones and have the other 3 people connect; **be sure to turn off data roaming on the U.S. primary SIMs** to avoid sky-high roaming charges.
- **Scenario 2｜The group stays together almost the whole time and rarely separates → Plan B**: book Pocket WiFi online in advance, pick it up at the airport, and share it for 3 days.

### Pitfalls to avoid
1. **Do not use U.S. carrier international roaming**: AT&T/Verizon $12/day/person, extremely expensive for 5 people over 3 days; T-Mobile premium plans may throttle easily when multiple people stream video.
2. **Hotspot**: one hotspot noticeably slows down with 3+ connected devices → **splitting traffic across 2 SIMs** gives a better experience.
3. **Taoyuan Airport free WiFi**: only good for temporary messaging, not stable; do not use it as the main connection.
4. **Payment**: airport SIM counters **accept international credit cards**, no need to exchange NT dollars in advance (**EasyCard top-ups are still cash-only**).

## 1. First do a 30-second device check
- **iPhone**: U.S. XS/XR and newer generally support eSIM and are usually unlocked; can install any eSIM below. Can store 8 eSIMs and enable 2 at the same time (U.S. SIM for SMS + travel SIM for data).

- Mainland China/Taiwan are both “foreign-network roaming” for you → the travel card needs **Data Roaming ON**; eSIM APN config is automatic, while Hong Kong physical SIMs may require manually entering APN `mobile.lte.three.com.hk`.

## 6. How early to buy / install / activate (key timeline)
**The only question that determines whether installing early wastes days: when does the validity period start?**
- **Timer starts on first connection / first use** → installing in advance is **100% safe, no days wasted**: **Airalo、Nomad、Saily、Roamless、GigSky** + **SIM World/3HK physical SIMs** (activate when used; shelf life 395/365 days).
- **Timer starts on activation (you choose the date)** → **Holafly**: install the profile in advance, but **enter the Mainland China arrival date at checkout and do not activate early**, otherwise days are wasted.
- **Ubigi/MobiMatter**: metered plans; MobiMatter explicitly says “install in advance, activate after arrival,” so treat as first-use timing but confirm at checkout.

**Recommended timeline:**
1. **Buy: 3–7 days before departure** (buy = receive QR/profile; buying early leaves buffer for payment/shipping issues; **Mainland China card must be purchased before crossing the border**).
2. **Install profile: 2–7 days before departure**, install at home on WiFi and name the line “Travel”; for first-use-timed cards, this step **does not** start the timer. (China-market Xiaomi with no EID: instead insert the Hong Kong physical SIM before departure and manually enter APN `mobile.lte.three.com.hk`)
3. **Before landing, keep the travel line “off”**; also turn **roaming/data off on U.S. SIMs** to prevent sky-high roaming charges.
4. **Landing in Taipei**: turn on the travel eSIM line + **Data Roaming ON** → the X-day timer starts only now (exactly when the trip begins); use it through the Mainland China segment.
5. **Holafly only**: timer starts on activation, do not activate early, enter the correct arrival date at checkout.
6. **Leave buffer**: validity period ≥ trip days. For a 3+7 day trip, choose a **10-day** tier (or SIM World 20GB/395-day card) for more stability.
