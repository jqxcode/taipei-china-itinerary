## 4. E-bike rental — two plans · 电动车租赁——两套方案

### Plan B — preferred, lower effort · 方案B（推荐，更省心）

Rent an e-bike directly in Shitang town, ideally right at the guesthouse door, or from a local shop in
town.

- **Route:** high-speed rail to Wenling Station → taxi to the Shitang guesthouse (reported ≈80 RMB,
  ≈45 min) → drop bags → rent e-bike at the guesthouse door → ride to the sights. *(For this itinerary,
  substitute "taxi direct from Hilton Taizhou, Jiaojiang" for the HSR leg — see §2 for the drive-time
  estimate.)*
- **Guesthouses reportedly offering on-site e-bike rental** (user-provided, **not independently verified
  — confirm on arrival**): 温岭石塘禧悦民宿, 吾家小院, 臻之小筑. Also recommended as a stay option near
  Xiaoruo: **琳朵艺术空间·海景美宿（石塘七彩小岛店）**, reportedly ~3 min walk to Dongdaozhu.
- **Local rental shops** (user-provided, **not independently verified**): 珂徕出行威哥租车电动车租赁 on
  马北路 (reportedly open 24h); 小王金箭电瓶车修理出租 on 冠平路 (reportedly 08:00–21:00). Web search
  confirms "珂徕出行" and similar e-bike rental services do operate in the Wenling/Shitang area generally,
  but did not independently confirm these two specific shop names/hours/addresses.
- **Bike spec:** recommend a **48V large-battery / hill-climbing model, 80–100 km range** — enough for
  the whole peninsula loop (Shitang's coastal sections are hilly).
- **Reported pricing** (confirm on the day): weekday ≈50 RMB/24h; weekend/peak ≈60 RMB/24h; hourly
  ≈12 RMB/h (not worth it vs. the day rate).
- **Rental process:** ID card required. Sesame Credit (芝麻信用分) ≥650 can waive the deposit; otherwise
  ≈200 RMB cash deposit, refunded in full on return if undamaged. **Inspect and photograph the bike's
  condition at pickup** (scratches, brakes, lights) to avoid disputes at return. Helmet + phone mount are
  usually included — **helmet is legally required, traffic police do check**.

### Plan A — alternative, no taxi needed · 方案A（备选，不需要打车）

For those who don't want a taxi and prefer riding the whole way from the train station.

- Rent at **绿津骑行·电动车租赁（温岭火车站店）** — web search confirms this is a real, currently
  operating e-bike rental brand with a Wenling train station location; reportedly ~450m/5 min walk from
  Wenling Station, good ratings, supports credit-based no-deposit rental. (Exact current pricing varies
  by source — treat the figure below as a starting point to confirm, not a locked quote.)
- **Reported pricing:** 2-day rental (battery-swap model, effectively unlimited range via free battery
  swaps) ≈120 RMB for 2 days — matches a 2-day/1-night itinerary.
- **Caveat:** the ~40 km Wenling Station→Shitang route runs on a national highway with more traffic —
  must choose a battery-swap/long-range model; a standard single-charge bike may not make it. The bike
  **must be returned to the same station-side shop** (cannot drop off in Shitang) — a real inconvenience
  for a one-way day-trip, which is why **Plan B is the primary recommendation** for this itinerary.

## 5. Safety / pitfall notes · 安全与避坑提示

1. **Always inspect + photograph brakes/body condition before riding off**, to avoid deposit disputes at
   return. 取车前务必检查并拍照记录刹车/车身状况，避免还车时押金纠纷。
2. **Helmet is legally required**, traffic police do check — you can get fined without one. 骑电动车必须
   戴头盔，交警会查，没戴会被罚。
3. **Choose a two-person/hill-climbing-capable model** — Shitang has some hilly coastal sections that an
   underpowered bike will struggle with. 选双人/爬坡款车型——石塘沿海路段有坡，动力不足的车会很吃力。
4. Duijie viewing platform requires a **~10–15 min walk up from the parking area with some steps** —
   non-slip shoes recommended (this is a bit more than the originally reported "short flat walk").
   对戒观景平台从停车场步行约10–15分钟上平台，部分路段有台阶，建议穿防滑鞋（比最初报告的"平缓短走"稍费力）。
