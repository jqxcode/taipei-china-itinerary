### Comparison: other Linhai Old Town / Ziyang Street chain hotels
If Atour is unavailable or price doesn't work, here are other researched chain-hotel alternatives (prices fluctuate by date — check Ctrip/Agoda live pricing before booking):
- **JI Hotel (Quanji, Chonghe / Yintai City branches)**: Huazhu Group brand, approx. RMB 350–550/night. Pro: solid standardized service at good value. Con: some branches are ~10 min by car from Ziyang Street.
- **Other Huazhu-family brands (Hanting, Crystal Orange, etc.)**: approx. RMB 250–350/night (Hanting economy) up to higher for Crystal Orange. Pro: affordable, many locations. Con: rooms/amenities generally smaller than Atour/JI Hotel.

### Comparison: Shitang Town / Xiaoruo Village (Wenling) overnight option
Shitang and Xiaoruo Village are a small coastal peninsula town — **no Atour/JI Hotel/Huazhu-family chain branches currently exist there**; the nearest chain hotels are in Wenling city proper (about 30–40 min drive). If overnighting in Shitang/Xiaoruo itself, the best-rated fallback options are boutique seaside guesthouses (民宿):
- **Ju Hai Shi Tang Seaview Resort Guesthouse**: No. 145 Donghai, Xiaoruo Village, Ruoshan, Shitang. Sea view straight out the window, near the harbor mouth, good for sunrise/sunset; has BBQ area, basketball court, public beach access, luggage storage — family-friendly.
- **Qicai Xiaowu (Rainbow Cottage) Seaview Guesthouse**: No. 157 Donghai, Xiaoruo Village. Approx. RMB 170–300/night (higher in peak season); the iconic "rainbow fishing village" photo spot; some units can be booked as a whole house.
- **Zhen Hai Ting Tao Guesthouse**: near the Millennium Dawn Park, excellent sunrise viewing, 270° sea view, some rooms with large terraces.
- **Ziluo Bay Sunrise Seaview Guesthouse**: near the south entrance of Dongxia Beach and the viewing platform, good sightlines, has tatami-style family rooms.
- If a standardized chain-hotel stay is preferred, book an **Atour Hotel** or **JI Hotel** in Wenling city and drive/taxi ~30–40 min to/from Shitang for Dongdaozhu Seafood, Xiaoruo Village, and the Duijie viewing platform.
