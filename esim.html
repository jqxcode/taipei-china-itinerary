Plan A【Most recommended · Flexible and independent, good for splitting up often】

  • Setup: 2 Chunghwa Telecom physical SIMs (buy on site at Taoyuan Airport).
  • Price: 3-day unlimited data NT$300/card (about US$10), includes a small calling credit and a local Taiwan +886 number.
  • Location: Taoyuan T1/T2 arrivals hall Chunghwa Telecom counter, handle it right after customs; just show passports (credit cards accepted).
  • How it works: 2 people carry SIM cards and can turn on hotspot anytime; the other 3 people connect to hotspot.

Final copy-this plan

  3. Taoyuan Airport free WiFi: only good for temporary messaging, not stable; do not use it as the main connection.
  4. Payment: airport SIM counters accept international credit cards, no need to exchange NT dollars in advance (EasyCard top-ups are still cash-only).

6. How early to buy / install / activate (key timeline)

The only question that determines whether installing early wastes days: when does the validity period start? - Timer starts on first connection / first use → installing in advance is 100% safe, no days wasted: Airalo、Nomad、Saily、Roamless、GigSky + SIM World/3HK physical SIMs (activate when used; shelf life 395/365 days). - Timer starts on activation (you choose the date) → Holafly: install the profile in advance, but enter the Mainland China arrival date at checkout and do not activate early, otherwise days are wasted. - Ubigi/MobiMatter: metered plans; MobiMatter explicitly says “install in advance, activate after arrival,” so treat as first-use timing but confirm at checkout.

Recommended timeline: 1. Buy: 3–7 days before departure (buy = receive QR/profile; buying early leaves buffer for payment/shipping issues; Mainland China card must be purchased before crossing the border). 2. Install profile: 2–7 days before departure, install at home on WiFi and name the line “Travel”; for first-use-timed cards, this step does not start the timer. (China-market Xiaomi with no EID: instead insert the Hong Kong physical SIM before departure and manually enter APN mobile.lte.three.com.hk) 3. Before landing, keep the travel line “off”; also turn roaming/data off on U.S. SIMs to prevent sky-high roaming charges. 4. Landing in Taipei: turn on the travel eSIM line + Data Roaming ON → the X-day timer starts only now (exactly when the trip begins); use it through the Mainland China segment. 5. Holafly only: timer starts on activation, do not activate early, enter the correct arrival date at checkout. 6. Leave buffer: validity period ≥ trip days. For a 3+7 day trip, choose a 10-day tier (or SIM World 20GB/395-day card) for more stability.
