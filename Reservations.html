Two, 🔴 Key Reminders

  • Closed on Mondays (mainland China): Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum, Memorial Hall of the Victims in Nanjing Massacre, and Presidential Palace are usually closed on Mondays. 9/14 is Monday → schedule these Nanjing sites for 9/15–9/17; on the day of arrival in mainland China, 9/14, prioritize Hangzhou West Lake/Longjing (not affected by Monday closure).
  • High-speed rail T-15 ticket rush: tickets for each segment go on sale 15 days before departure; popular G trains may sell out within hours → buy on 12306/Trip.com as soon as sales open (each segment begins opening gradually from about 8/30).
  • General strategy for mainland China foreigner real-name reservations: ① Trip.com (English + passport + international card, covers most attractions); ② WeChat mini program: choose the “passport” document type (Lingyin Temple clearly supports this); ③ for the person with the Mainland Travel Permit for Taiwan Residents, choose the “Mainland Travel Permit for Taiwan Residents” document type for smoother booking; ④ backup: ask the hotel concierge to book on your behalf (especially for Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum/Memorial Hall).

Three, 👵 Senior Travel Notes

  • Passport holders age 70+: Lingyin Temple, Humble Administrator's Garden, Tiger Hill often offer free admission (explain at ticket purchase/entry).
  • Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum has many steps, and Humble Administrator's Garden/Tiger Hill can have slippery paving → allow enough time and pace according to ability; the Memorial Hall involves a lot of walking, so morning entry is preferable.
  • For high-speed rail, choose aisle seats if possible; major stations have accessible routes.
