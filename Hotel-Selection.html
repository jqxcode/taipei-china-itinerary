Comparison: Shitang Town / Xiaoruo Village (Wenling) overnight option

Shitang and Xiaoruo Village are a small coastal peninsula town — no Atour/JI Hotel/Huazhu-family chain branches currently exist there; the nearest chain hotels are in Wenling city proper (about 30–40 min drive). If overnighting in Shitang/Xiaoruo itself, the best-rated fallback options are boutique seaside guesthouses (民宿): - Ju Hai Shi Tang Seaview Resort Guesthouse: No. 145 Donghai, Xiaoruo Village, Ruoshan, Shitang. Sea view straight out the window, near the harbor mouth, good for sunrise/sunset; has BBQ area, basketball court, public beach access, luggage storage — family-friendly. - Qicai Xiaowu (Rainbow Cottage) Seaview Guesthouse: No. 157 Donghai, Xiaoruo Village. Approx. RMB 170–300/night (higher in peak season); the iconic "rainbow fishing village" photo spot; some units can be booked as a whole house. - Zhen Hai Ting Tao Guesthouse: near the Millennium Dawn Park, excellent sunrise viewing, 270° sea view, some rooms with large terraces. - Ziluo Bay Sunrise Seaview Guesthouse: near the south entrance of Dongxia Beach and the viewing platform, good sightlines, has tatami-style family rooms. - If a standardized chain-hotel stay is preferred, book an Atour Hotel or JI Hotel in Wenling city and drive/taxi ~30–40 min to/from Shitang for Dongdaozhu Seafood, Xiaoruo Village, and the Duijie viewing platform.
