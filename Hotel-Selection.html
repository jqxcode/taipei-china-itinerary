Hotel Booking Plan (Plan SH: Taipei + East China: Shanghai / Hangzhou / Nanjing / Suzhou)

Applies to: family of 5, 2 rooms in each city; Mom values comfort more; September 2026. (Linked from Master-Plan.md · the HTML itinerary’s “Lodging” links to the corresponding city sections in this file.) Itinerary is locked to Plan SH: Taipei (3 nights) → Shanghai → Hangzhou → Nanjing → Suzhou → Shanghai (final leg). The old “Route C Xiamen/Quanzhou/Fuzhou/Chongqing route” has been discarded and is no longer kept in this file. For the mainland China portion, prioritize Hilton-family hotels (Hilton / Conrad / Waldorf Astoria / DoubleTree / Hampton / Canopy) and use Hilton Diamond + Aspire free-night cert to maximize value; Palais de Chine in Taipei is not Hilton (see below), so pay separately with cash/points. Prices are rough pre-tax nightly rates per room visible on web pages (estimated, pulled 2026-08). Before final booking, you must re-check real-time 2026-09 prices, cancellation policies, bed types, and connecting rooms.

One-line conclusion

  • Taipei: choose 君品酒店 Palais de Chine Hotel (Taipei Main Station (台北车站)/Q Square (京站)) — convenient for late-night arrival, fast to National Palace Museum/101/Ximen/Songshan, comfortable for elders. Not Hilton; cash is about NT$9,800–13,500/night.
  • Shanghai: use the Aspire free-night cert first at Waldorf Astoria on the Bund (highest cash rate, strongest experience); baseline option is Conrad Shanghai. The cert only covers 1 standard double-occupancy room for 1 night; book the second room with cash/points.
  • Hangzhou: for West Lake routing, first choice is Canopy by Hilton Hangzhou West Lake; for better hardware, choose Conrad Hangzhou.
  • Nanjing: full-service first choice is Hilton Nanjing (Jianye · near Xinjiekou (新街口)); if you want to stay close to Confucius Temple/Qinhuai River, choose Hampton by Hilton Nanjing Confucius Temple (breakfast included, good value).
  • Suzhou: flagship Hilton Suzhou (Jinji Lake · Industrial Park) (family-friendly, metro into the old city); if you want to be closer to Pingjiang Road, choose Hampton by Hilton Suzhou Guanqian Street (breakfast included, closest Hilton-family option to the old city).

Free-night cert and Diamond strategy (mainland China Hilton stays only)

  1. Aspire free-night cert → prioritize Waldorf Astoria on the Bund in Shanghai (1 room for 1 night, highest cash value). Rules: redeemable for standard accommodation, double occupancy, subject to standard reward room availability, must be redeemed by phone, and cannot be converted to cash/points. With 5 people, you still need a second room with cash/points; add the same-order note family traveling together, connecting rooms.
  2. Do not budget as if Diamond breakfast/lounge will cover all 5 people for free: full-service hotels usually only cover “member +1”. Book both rooms through official Hilton channels; if there is a second Hilton Gold/Diamond account, attaching the second room to that account is safest; Hampton/Home2 with breakfast included are simpler.
  3. Points: Hilton uses dynamic pricing — when WA/Conrad cash rates are high and standard reward rooms exist → consider points/cert; when Hampton/HGI/DoubleTree cash rates are cheap → cash is better and saves points; do not use the cert at Hampton/DoubleTree (unless it is about to expire).
  4. Palais de Chine Taipei is not Hilton: Hilton benefits do not apply at all; use cash or transferable points (Capital One, etc.), or book directly with cash; compare Trip.com/Booking/official site before booking.

免费房券与 Diamond 策略（仅大陆 Hilton 段）

  1. Aspire 免费房券 → 优先上海外滩华尔道夫（1 间 1 晚，现金价值最高）。规则：可兑标准住宿、双人入住、视标准奖励房供应、需电话兑换、不可换现金/点数。5 人仍需第二间房现金/点数，同订单备注 family traveling together, connecting rooms。
  2. Diamond 早餐/酒廊别按 5 人全免预算：全服务酒店通常只免「会员+1」。两间房都走 Hilton 官方；若有第二个 Hilton Gold/Diamond 账号，第二间挂它最稳；Hampton/Home2 含早更省心。
  3. 点数：Hilton 动态定价——WA/Conrad 现金贵且有标准奖励房→可点数/房券；Hampton/HGI/DoubleTree 现金便宜→现金更划算、留点数；房券别用在 Hampton/DoubleTree（除非快过期）。
  4. 台北君品非 Hilton：Hilton 权益全不适用；用现金或可转点(Capital One 等)，或直接现金订；订前比 Trip.com/Booking/官网。

🏨 Shanghai · Use cert at Bund WA, Conrad as baseline

🏨 上海 · 外滩 WA 用券，Conrad 基础盘

  • Waldorf Astoria on the Bund（高光·用房券）：外滩位置+历史建筑+服务，Aspire 免费房券用在这（1 间 1 晚）；第二间现金/点数同订。约 ¥2,200–2,800（≈US$325–414）。

🏨 Hangzhou · Canopy for West Lake routing / Conrad for hardware

🏨 Nanjing · Full-service Hilton / Hampton close to Confucius Temple

🏨 南京 · 全服务 Hilton / 夫子庙贴身 Hampton

🏨 Suzhou · Jinji Lake flagship Hilton / Hampton closest to old city

  • Hilton Suzhou (first choice · Jinji Lake · Suzhou Industrial Park): family-friendly, pool, well-reviewed rooms/breakfast; ~9–10km from Pingjiang Road (20–30 min by taxi or metro into the old city). About ¥700–1,000（≈US$108–155）.

  • Note: in 2026, there is no DoubleTree/Canopy in the Suzhou old city/Pingjiang Road area; DoubleTree is in Wujiang (farther south). Only 1 night → if the priority is walking Pingjiang Road, choose Hampton Guanqian Street for proximity; if the priority is the flagship experience, choose Hilton Jinji Lake. If you want to “stay inside the old city,” also compare non-Hilton boutique guesthouses around Pingjiang Road.

Booking action checklist

  1. Search each city for 2026-09 using 2 rooms and the true party size of 5 people.
  2. Prioritize official Hilton channels (to reliably receive Diamond benefits/points/cert); use Trip.com/Booking only for price comparison. Palais de Chine Taipei is not Hilton, so compare and book directly via official site/Trip.com/Booking.
  3. Add this note to every Hilton booking: Family of 5 traveling together / Please assign two rooms nearby or connecting / Diamond member; breakfast consideration for family.
  4. 3–5 days before arrival, confirm by app/email: bed types for both rooms, connecting/same-floor arrangement, number of guests covered for breakfast, children’s policy, and whether the lounge is open.
  5. Free-night cert: first check whether WA Shanghai has Standard Room Reward availability → if yes, redeem by phone; book the second room at the same hotel for the same night with cash.
  6. Cancellation policy: September China-city rates fluctuate a lot, so first book cancellable rates; after rail/flights are locked, then switch to non-refundable rates if worthwhile.

🏨 苏州 · 金鸡湖旗舰 Hilton / 古城最近 Hampton

  • Hilton Suzhou（首选·金鸡湖·苏州工业园区）：家庭友好、泳池、房间/早餐口碑好；离平江路 ~9–10km(打车 20–30 分或地铁进古城)。约 ¥700–1,000（≈US$108–155）。
  • Hampton by Hilton Suzhou Guanqian Street（备选·观前街）：离平江路最近的 Hilton 系(~4km)、含早、中端；想步行古城动线更顺可选它。约 ¥400–600（≈US$62–93）。
  • 说明：2026 年苏州古城/平江路一带无 DoubleTree/Canopy；DoubleTree 在吴江(偏南)。仅 1 晚→若重逛平江路选 Hampton 观前街更近；重旗舰体验选 Hilton 金鸡湖。想要「住进古城」也可另比非 Hilton 的平江路精品民宿。

订房操作清单

  1. 每城按 2 间房、5 人真实人数 搜 2026-09。
  2. Hilton 官方优先（才稳拿 Diamond/积分/房券）；Trip.com/Booking 只比价。台北君品非 Hilton，直接官网/Trip.com/Booking 比价订。
  3. 每个 Hilton 订单备注：Family of 5 traveling together / Please assign two rooms nearby or connecting / Diamond member; breakfast consideration for family。
  4. 到店前 3–5 天 APP/邮件确认：两间房床型、连通/同层、早餐覆盖人数、儿童政策、酒廊是否开放。
  5. 免费房券：先查 WA Shanghai 有无 Standard Room Reward→有则电话兑换；第二间同晚现金订同酒店。
  6. 取消政策：9 月中国城市价波动大，先订可取消价；高铁/航班锁定后再换不可取消价。
