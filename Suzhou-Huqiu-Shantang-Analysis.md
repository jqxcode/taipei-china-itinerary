## Huqiu assessment

- **Minimum:** 75–90 minutes; core approach, Thousand-People Stone/Sword Pool vicinity and pagoda view, aggressively skipping branches.
- **Comfortable:** 2–2.5 hours.
- **Full:** 3.5–4 hours.
- Despite only roughly **34 m of hill relief**, the core route has sloping stone paving, uneven surfaces and repeated stairs. Sword Pool and upper historic features are not reliably wheelchair-accessible.
- A sightseeing cart may reduce peripheral approach walking but should **not** be treated as an elevator to every core sight. Boat operations do not remove the hill climb.
- Planning allowance: approximately **2.5–4 km inside**, depending on routing.
- September planning hours commonly published: **07:30–17:30**, last admission earlier; peak-season adult ticket commonly **¥70**. Reconfirm in the official **苏州园林旅游/君到苏州** channel because 2026 cart routes, last entry and concessions are not yet reliably published on an accessible official webpage.
- Tiger Hill’s heritage value is real, but it adds **2–2.5 hours onsite plus 45–70 minutes of detour transport** to this particular east-old-city itinerary.

## Shantang assessment

- The street itself is free and effectively always accessible; shops generally operate approximately 09:00–21:00/22:00.
- **Minimum dinner stroll:** 75–90 minutes, limiting Mom to the photogenic eastern 300–600 m.
- **Comfortable:** 2–2.5 hours including seated dinner and a roughly 35–45-minute canal cruise.
- **Full:** 3.5–4.5 hours; the historic “seven-li” axis is about 3.5 km one way and is inappropriate for this low-walking day.
- Best arrival on September 14: **17:20–17:40**, eat before peak demand, then see blue hour/lights around **18:00–19:30**.
- Densest crowds normally occur around **19:00–21:00**, especially at bridges and the eastern commercial core. Monday should help but does not guarantee quiet.
- Commonly listed cruise planning envelope is approximately **09:00–20:30/21:00**, about **¥50–60**, but exact September 2026 departures must be confirmed in the official 七里山塘/君到苏州 channel. Do not assume the sightseeing cruise is through transportation to Huqiu.
